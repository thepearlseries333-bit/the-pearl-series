📕 D · Novel — The Old Man and the Sea

Exercises on the Novel 10 Q

1Hemingway used Santiago's eyes to show his inner spirit. Discuss giving your opinion.
Although Santiago is old and his body is worn out, his eyes are described as cheerful and undefeated, the same colour as the sea. This shows that his spirit is still young, strong and full of hope. In my opinion, Hemingway used the eyes to tell us that real strength comes from inside, not from the body.
2In what ways does Santiago represent courage even before the big journey begins?
He has gone 84 days without catching a fish, yet he never gives up hope. He keeps his sail rolled, prepares his lines carefully and plans to go far out into the sea alone. Facing failure and poverty with a calm, confident heart is itself an act of great courage.
3The villagers think that Santiago was unlucky. Do you think they were right? Why?
No, I don't think they were right. Santiago was not unlucky; he was simply going through a hard time. He was skilful, patient and experienced, and later he proved this by catching the greatest fish any of them had ever seen.
4The boy spent his evenings with Santiago. Comment giving your reasons.
Manolin loved and respected Santiago, who had taught him how to fish. He brought him food, coffee and bait, listened to his talk about baseball and gave him company. Their friendship shows loyalty and gratitude that go beyond age.
5In what way does Santiago's hut reflect his personality?
The hut is very simple: a bed, a table, a chair and a picture of his late wife. It shows that Santiago is a poor but clean, humble and dignified man who keeps his memories close and needs very little to live.
6When did Santiago wake up?
He woke up very early, before sunrise, while it was still dark.
7Why did Santiago love this hour in the morning?
Because it was quiet and peaceful, the sea was calm, and it gave him a feeling of hope and a fresh start to the day.
8What did Manolin give Santiago?
He gave him coffee, food (and sardines/bait) before he set out to sea.
9How did the fishermen react towards Santiago?
Some of the older fishermen respected him and felt sorry for him, while the younger ones made fun of him because he had caught no fish for a long time.
10What did Santiago remember while he was rowing?
He remembered Africa, the lions playing on the beaches at sunset, and the days of his youth and strength.

A Strange Day in My Life
I will never forget one strange day last winter. I woke up late and rushed to school without breakfast. On my way, I found a small leather wallet lying on the pavement. Inside it, there was a large sum of money and an identity card.
For a moment, I felt confused. Then I remembered my father's advice: "Honesty is the best policy." I went straight to the nearest police station and handed the wallet to the officer. He smiled, took my name and thanked me warmly.
In the evening, there was a knock at our door. It was the owner of the wallet, an elderly man who had come with the police officer. He hugged me and told my parents that the money was for his daughter's operation. My father's eyes shone with pride.
That strange day taught me a lesson I will keep forever: a small honest act can change someone's whole life.
