📕 D · The Novel

The Old Man and the Sea — Answer the following questions 3 Q

1How does Hemingway use the bird to symbolize guidance and hope?
The small tired bird that lands on Santiago's line shows him where the fish are, so it acts as a guide at sea. It also keeps him company in his loneliness and reminds him that he is not alone, which fills him with hope and courage to go on.
2Why does Santiago speak to the fish as if it were a human?
Because he respects the marlin as a noble and brave opponent and feels a deep bond with it. Being alone for so long, he talks to the fish to keep himself company and calls it his brother, showing his love for nature and his belief that man and fish share the same struggle.
3What does Santiago's refusal to surrender reveal about his values?
It shows his courage, patience and dignity. He believes that a man can be destroyed but not defeated. Even when his hands bleed and his body is exhausted, he refuses to give up because pride, endurance and determination are more important to him than the fish itself.
